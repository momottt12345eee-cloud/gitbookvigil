# Feedback and Support

## Where to reach us

**Telegram group** — the main channel during the Closed Beta. The whole team is there and reads every message: bugs, questions, ideas, and "wait, can Vigil do this?"

See [Community & Links](../community/community-and-links.md).

## A great bug report

Include whatever you can:

1. **What you asked for** — your plan, in your words
2. **What you expected**
3. **What happened**
4. **When** — approximate time and time zone, and which market
5. **A screenshot or recording** — even a phone photo helps

A two-line message like "my ETH plan stopped responding around 02:30 UTC, screenshot attached" beats a perfect report that never gets sent.

## What we want most

Bug reports matter. But the most useful feedback is the stuff you'd normally keep to yourself:

* The plan you wanted but couldn't figure out how to phrase
* The moment Vigil surprised you, good or bad
* The thing you expected Vigil to do that it didn't
* The part of your routine Vigil could take off your hands

Half-formed ideas are welcome.

## What happens next

* **Bugs** are checked every day. Critical ones are fixed first.
* **Product feedback** is reviewed weekly and shapes the roadmap.
* **Feature requests** are tracked. We won't build everything, but we'll tell you what we're doing and why.

The first Beta group will have an outsized say in what Vigil becomes. Thank you for being here early.
