# How to Join

**The Vigil Closed Beta is invite-only.**

We're keeping the first group small. Right now, the quality of feedback matters more than the number of sign-ups.

## If you've been invited

You'll receive an invite link directly from the team, with any extra setup steps.

Go straight to [Quick Start](quick-start.md).

## If you haven't been invited yet

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Follow Vigil and join the community

Invite waves are announced on X and in Telegram first. See [Community & Links](../community/community-and-links.md). The team is active in the chat and notices who shows up.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Ask a current Beta user

Once a Beta user is set up and active, they can invite a small number of people.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## How invites grow

When a new user joins, connects their wallet and runs their first plan, they unlock a few invites of their own.

This is on purpose. We'd rather grow through traders bringing in traders they trust than through a long list of accounts that never run a plan.

**Who we're looking for:** people who already have a plan and want it carried out, day and night.

{% hint style="danger" %}
The team will **never** DM you first, ask for your seed phrase or private keys, or ask you to send funds to "unlock" access.
{% endhint %}
