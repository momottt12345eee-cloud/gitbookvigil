## Monthly goal

Each month, meet **one** of these:

* Bring in 5 or more active trading users, or
* Be an active trading user yourself

{% hint style="info" %}
**Active trading user** = a wallet that has funded at least $100, still holds $100+ after 14 days, and has real trading volume within 30 days.
{% endhint %}

Content and events are encouraged. There's no minimum count for either.

## Day to day

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Self-report** | Every two weeks, a short form with your content links and event info. Under two minutes. |
| **Spot checks** | We review a sample of submitted content. |
| **User activity** | Tracked automatically. You don't report it. |

If you miss the monthly goal, we'll check in first to see what's in the way. If it's missed two months in a row, Sentinel perks are paused and the badge is removed.

## What you get

* **Referral earnings** — a share of fees from users you bring in, at a higher Sentinel rate
* **Rewards for market insight** — good calls and research are recognized
* **Bonuses** for standout months
* **Priority access** to new features and the product team

## Talking about Vigil

You're representing Vigil in public. A few rules matter.

**Please do:**

* ✅ Share your real experience and real trade records
* ✅ Show what the product does and how it works
* ✅ Disclose the partnership — always tag Vigil's handle

**Please don't:**

* ❌ Promise returns or suggest guaranteed results
* ❌ Make trading decisions for other people
* ❌ Overstate what the agent can do
* ❌ Post low-effort, AI-generated filler

People trade real money with Vigil. Overselling it doesn't just hurt the brand, it puts your audience at risk. When in doubt, say less and show more.
