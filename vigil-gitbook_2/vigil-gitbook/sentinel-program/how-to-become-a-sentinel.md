# How to Become a Sentinel

<!-- TODO: confirm thresholds and the application link. -->

Everyone who applies goes through a review. Only people who pass become Sentinels. Applying alone doesn't make you one.

## The three requirements

You need all three. There's no interview.

### 1. Bring real users

Two of your invites used, with each person funding their wallet and running a first plan. We count real activity, not empty sign-ups.

### 2. Post on your own channels

At least two original Vigil posts on your X account or in your community, tagged with Vigil's handle, with real engagement (roughly 1,000+ views and 5+ genuine replies each).

### 3. Help the official channels grow

Bring real people to follow Vigil on X or join the Telegram, through a trackable link.
