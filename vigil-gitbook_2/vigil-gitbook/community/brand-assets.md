## Icons

Vigil uses a **sun** for daytime moments and a **crescent moon** for night moments, both drawn in thin amber line. Together they stand for 24/7.

## Usage rules

**Do:**

* Use the original files
* Keep clear space around the logo, at least the height of the eye mark
* Scale proportionally
* Place the logo on a background with enough contrast

**Don't:**

* Recolor it, or add gradients, shadows or effects
* Stretch, squash or rotate it
* Rebuild the wordmark in another typeface
* Crop it or use part of it on its own
* Place it on a busy background where it's hard to read
* Suggest a partnership or endorsement that doesn't exist

Not sure if your use is OK? Ask us — see [Community & Links](community-and-links.md).
