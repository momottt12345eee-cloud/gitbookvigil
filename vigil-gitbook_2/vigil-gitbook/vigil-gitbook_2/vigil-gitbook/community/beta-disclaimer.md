## Trading risk

Trading digital assets carries a high level of risk. You can lose some or all of your funds.

* Only trade with money you can afford to lose.
* Start small while you learn how Vigil reads your plans.
* Don't assume any system, including Vigil, will protect you from losses.

## Vigil is not financial advice

Nothing in Vigil, this documentation, the Vigil website, or any message from the Vigil team is financial, investment, legal or tax advice. Vigil is a software tool that carries out plans **you** write and approve. The decisions, the trades and the outcomes are yours.

We are not a registered financial advisor, broker, dealer or fiduciary. We don't manage your money, and we don't recommend what to trade.

If you need financial advice, talk to a qualified professional where you live.

## Automation risk

Automated trading can fail because of fast markets, low liquidity, slippage, network congestion, exchange outages or software errors. Watches check on a cadence, not tick by tick, so a fast move can be missed.

## Token risk

The Vigil token is not live yet. It is scheduled to launch before the Closed Beta opens. When it launches:

* It is a way to pay for access to Vigil, not an investment product.
* Its price can go up or down, and it can lose value. Burning tokens does not guarantee any price.
* Token plans, including supply, the burn split and launch timing, may change before launch.
* Rules on digital tokens differ by country. Check that you're allowed to hold and use it where you live.
