## Why a token

Vigil is a product people pay for every month. The token turns that monthly usage into something simple and transparent:

* **One way to pay.** Access to Vigil is paid in the token.
* **Usage-driven burn.** Most of every payment is permanently destroyed, not kept.
* **Funds for building.** A small, fixed share goes to the project so Vigil can keep running and improving.

## How it works in one picture

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Use Vigil (from the first day of the Beta) | Each account uses **100 USDT worth** of tokens per month |
| 2. Split | Every payment is split automatically: **90%** and **10%** |
| 3. Burn | **90%** is sent to a burn address and removed from supply forever |
| 4. Build | **10%** goes to the project treasury for development and operations |
