# Token FAQ

<details>

<summary>Is the token live?</summary>

Not yet. The token launches before the Closed Beta opens, and Beta access is paid in it from day one. Launch details will only be announced on our [official channels](../community/community-and-links.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>How much does Vigil cost with the token?</summary>

100 USDT worth of tokens per account, per month. The dollar value is fixed; the number of tokens depends on the price at billing time. See [Access and Usage](access-and-usage.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>What happens to the tokens I pay?</summary>

90% is burned permanently. 10% goes to the project treasury to fund development and operations. See [Burn and Treasury](burn-and-treasury.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can the team change the 90 / 10 split?</summary>

The split is set in the payment contract. Any future change would be announced in advance on official channels and in these docs.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What if I don't have enough tokens on my billing date?</summary>

Your watches pause until you top up. Nothing is deleted, and Vigil never sells your other assets to cover the cost.

</details>

<details>

</details>

No. The token is how you pay for Vigil. Nothing in these docs is a promise of price, profit or return, and nothing here is financial advice.

</details>
