## How Vigil thinks

Every plan in Vigil follows the same loop:

1. **Say it** — write your plan in plain words
2. **It watches** — prices, listings, wallets and volume, 24/7
3. **It acts** — only inside the limits you set, or after you confirm
4. **It reports** — every fill, skip and pause is logged with the reason
