# About the Sentinel Program

<!-- TODO: confirm rewards, cohorts and application link. -->

**Every market move has someone who saw it first.**

The Sentinel Program is Vigil's ambassador program. We're looking for traders, researchers and creators who spot things early, and who are genuinely into what AI agents can do for traders.

Sentinels get early access, a direct line to the team, and a hand in shaping 24/7 trading. In return, they help more traders find Vigil through content and events.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Applications are open.** See [How to Become a Sentinel](how-to-become-a-sentinel.md).
{% endhint %}

## What Sentinels get

* **Early Beta access** ahead of the wider waitlist
* **An official Sentinel badge**, announced from Vigil's X account
* **Rewards for good calls** — your market insight counts, not just your reach
* **Referral earnings** — details in the [Sentinel Handbook](sentinel-handbook.md)
* **Bonuses** for standout contributions
* **A priority channel** to the product team

## Why "Sentinel"

A sentinel is the one on watch while everyone else rests. It's the same job Vigil does for your trades. Sentinels are the people who keep watch for their community, and pass on what they see.

## Two tracks

You can apply to one or both.

### Track A — Content

Create and share content on X, Telegram, YouTube, TikTok and similar platforms. We look at reach and real sign-ups, with referral earnings on top.

**Good fit:** crypto or AI creators, trading influencers, educators.

### Track B — Events

Host meetups, workshops and live trading sessions that bring in active users. We support you with brand assets, event funding and speakers.

**Good fit:** community organizers, meetup hosts, campus groups, event producers.

## Where we're recruiting

The first group — the **First Watch** cohort — is recruited by language community, starting with English and Chinese.
