# What is Vigil

**Vigil is a 24/7 AI trading agent.**

**Trading that never sleeps.**

## What Vigil is

* An agent that runs **your** plan, written in your own words
* A system that shows you exactly what it will do before it does anything
* A watcher that stays on duty through every session, weekend and time zone
* A tool where every trigger, decision and fill is logged and explainable

## What Vigil is not

* A signal group or a "hot picks" feed
* A copy-trading product
* A fund or a managed account
* A black box that "trades for you" without telling you why
* A chatbot you have to keep awake with new commands
