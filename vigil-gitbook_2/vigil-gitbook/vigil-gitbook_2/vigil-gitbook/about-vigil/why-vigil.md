# Why Vigil

Good trading needs three things at once: a clear plan, fast execution and steady discipline. Most people can manage one or two. Almost nobody manages all three, around the clock, on their own.

## The problem

You already know how this goes.

* You set an alarm for a 3 AM level and sleep through it.
* A new token matches everything you look for while you're in a meeting.
* You take two losses, size up to win it back, and make it worse.
* You spot a whale wallet moving, copy it by hand, and arrive late.

None of this means your ideas are bad. It means one person can't watch every chart, every hour, with a calm head.

## Why teams win and individuals burn out

Professional desks split the job: someone researches, someone sets risk limits, someone executes, someone watches positions overnight, someone reviews what happened. That loop never stops, because it's shared across people and shifts.

An individual trader has to be all of those people. While also working. While also sleeping.

## What Vigil does about it

Vigil takes the night shift, and the day shift.

You keep the thesis and the final say. Vigil turns your words into a precise plan, keeps watch 24/7, acts when your conditions are met, and tells you exactly what happened and why.

You're not giving your trading away. You're giving it a guard that doesn't get tired.

## What changes

* **You stop missing setups.** The agent was awake even when you weren't.
* **Your rules hold under pressure.** The limits you set while calm are the limits that run when the market moves.
* **You can hold a view for weeks.** No more watching every candle to protect a position.
* **You can act on more ideas.** Each one costs minutes to set up, not hours of attention.

The point isn't to trade more. It's to make sure every trade is one you actually decided on.
