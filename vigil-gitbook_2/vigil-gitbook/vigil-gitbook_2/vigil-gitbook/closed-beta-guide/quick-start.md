{% step %}
### Create your account

Sign in with email or a social account. Your wallet is set up for you.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Fund your wallet

Tap **Fund wallet**, copy your address, and send USDC from any wallet or exchange.

Start small. You can always add more later.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Set your guardrails

Before your first plan, set:

* Max size per trade
* Max loss per day
* Default mode — we recommend **Confirm** ("Ask me first") to start
* How often watches check — every 1, 5 or 15 minutes

See [Managing Your Watches](managing-your-watches.md).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Write your first plan

Tap the input box and describe what you want, in your own words:

> Buy $50 of SOL if it drops below 140. Take profit at 155, stop at 134.

Vigil turns it into a **plan card** with every detail:

* Trigger
* Action and size
* Stop and take profit
* Market it will run on
* Mode (Confirm or Auto)
* A plain-language risk summary

Read the card carefully. This is the moment to catch a misunderstanding. Edit anything that looks off, or rewrite your plan.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Confirm

When the card looks right, tap **Confirm**. Your watch is live, 24/7. You can close the app.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
