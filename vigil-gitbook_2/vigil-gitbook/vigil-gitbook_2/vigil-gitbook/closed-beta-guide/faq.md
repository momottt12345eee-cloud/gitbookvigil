# FAQ

<details>

<summary>Is Vigil open to everyone?</summary>

Not yet. Vigil is invite-only during the Closed Beta. See [How to Join](how-to-join.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>What can Vigil trade today?</summary>

<!-- TODO: list real venues and chains -->

See the **Markets** section in [What Vigil Can Do](../about-vigil/what-vigil-can-do.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Who controls my funds?</summary>

<!-- TODO: confirm custody wording -->

Your assets stay in your own wallet. Vigil has no pooled account and doesn't combine user funds.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can Vigil withdraw or send my funds somewhere else?</summary>

No. Vigil has trading permissions only. There is no path for it to send your funds to another address.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Do I need to keep the app open?</summary>

No. Once you confirm a plan, the watch runs 24/7 whether the app is open or not.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can Vigil trade without my approval?</summary>

No plan starts until you confirm its plan card. After that, it runs in the mode you chose: **Confirm** asks before every order, **Auto** acts inside your caps.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I change or stop a watch?</summary>

Yes, at any time. Edit, pause or stop it from the watch screen, or use **Pause all**. See [Managing Your Watches](managing-your-watches.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>What's the safest way to start?</summary>

Start with an **alert-only** plan. Then try a simple plan with a small size in **Confirm** mode. Don't start with complex, leveraged plans on day one.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Something looks broken. What should I do?</summary>

Report it right away with what you asked for, what you expected, what happened, when, and a screenshot. See [Feedback and Support](feedback-and-support.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Is there a Vigil token?</summary>

Not yet. The Vigil token launches before the Closed Beta opens. From the first day of the Beta, access costs 100 USDT worth of tokens per month, with 90% burned and 10% going to the project. See [Token Overview](../token/token-overview.md).

Until we announce it on official channels, any token or contract claiming to be Vigil's is not from us.

</details>
