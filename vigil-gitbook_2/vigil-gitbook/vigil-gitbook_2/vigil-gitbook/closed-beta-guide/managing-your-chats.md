# Managing Your Chats

<!-- TODO: confirm UI labels against the live app. -->

You can talk to Vigil in more than one chat, for example one for research and one per strategy. What Vigil knows about you, like your usual size and the markets you follow, carries across all chats. The conversation itself stays inside each chat.

## Starting a chat

Tap **New chat** at the top of the chat list.

## Finding your chats

* **Pinned** — chats you've pinned, in your own order
* **Recent** — everything else, newest first

Each chat shows how many watches are running in it, and a dot if there's something you haven't read.

## Pin, rename, archive

* **Web** — hover a chat to pin or archive it; double-click the title to rename it.
* **App** — press and hold a chat for the same options.

Archived chats leave your list. If you need one back, ask the team in Telegram.

## Archiving a chat with running watches

A chat can only be archived when none of its watches are running. If some are, Vigil offers **Stop all and archive**. This only stops watches in that chat.

## Switching chats

Switching never interrupts anything:

* Watches keep running and report back to the chat they were created in.
* Unsent drafts stay where you typed them.
* Tapping a notification opens the right chat.

Your chats stay in sync across web and mobile.
