# Subscriptions During the Beta

<!-- TODO: confirm plan names, payment providers and test-card details before publishing. -->

Every Closed Beta member gets Vigil's paid features for free while the Beta lasts.

## Nothing is charged

During the Beta, subscriptions run in a **test environment**. No real money is taken, and no real card is billed.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Beta subscriptions don't carry over.** When Vigil launches publicly, test subscriptions end. If you want a paid plan after launch, you'll subscribe again then.
{% endhint %}

## After the Beta

When the Vigil token launches, access will be paid in the token: **100 USDT worth per month**, with 90% burned and 10% going to the project treasury. See [Access and Usage](../token/access-and-usage.md).

## How to activate

1. Go to **Settings → Plan**.
2. Choose the plan you want.
3. Follow the checkout. Use the test details your invite provides, not a real card.

## Known issues

* **Activation can take a few seconds.** If your plan hasn't changed, wait a moment or restart the app.
* **Some failures are silent.** If your plan still hasn't changed after a restart, send us your user ID in Telegram.

{% hint style="danger" %}
We will **never** ask for your passwords, seed phrase or real card details. If anyone does, it isn't us. Tell us right away.
{% endhint %}
