# Access During the Beta

<!-- TODO: confirm the token ticker, supported wallets and billing date rules before publishing. -->

The Vigil token launches **before** the Closed Beta opens. From the first day of the Beta, access to Vigil is paid in the token.

## What it costs

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly access | **100 USDT worth** of the Vigil token, per account |
| Where it goes | **90% burned**, **10%** to the project treasury |
| How it's priced | Fixed in dollars. The number of tokens depends on the price at billing time |

Full details: [Access and Usage](../token/access-and-usage.md) and [Burn and Treasury](../token/burn-and-treasury.md).

{% hint style="info" %}
**You still need an invite.** Holding the token doesn't give you access on its own. The Closed Beta stays invite-only. See [How to Join](how-to-join.md).
{% endhint %}

## How to activate

1. Hold enough Vigil tokens in your connected wallet to cover one month.
2. Go to **Settings → Plan**.
3. Check the exact token amount shown, then approve the monthly payment.
4. Your access renews each month on the same date.

## If a payment fails

* Your watches are **paused, not deleted**.
* Top up your tokens and they resume where they left off.
* Vigil never sells your other assets to cover the cost.

## Known issues

* **Activation can take a few seconds.** If your plan hasn't changed, wait a moment or restart the app.
* **Some failures are silent.** If your plan still hasn't changed after a restart, send us your user ID in [Telegram](https://t.me/vigilweb3).

{% hint style="danger" %}
Vigil only ever asks you to approve the exact monthly amount, inside the app. We will **never** ask for your passwords or seed phrase, or ask you to send tokens to a personal wallet or a DM link. If anyone does, it isn't us. Tell us right away.
{% endhint %}
